Context Cards - Legal Sources

What the law says

Under the Lagos State Tenancy Law 2011, your landlord cannot evict you without proper notice. Section 13 requires a minimum of 7 days notice for monthly tenants and 6 months notice for yearly tenants.

Furthermore, Section 20 prohibits self-help eviction (such as changing locks or removing your belongings). Only a court order can authorize eviction.

A landlord shall not evict a tenant without giving proper notice to quit. For monthly tenancies, the notice period shall be seven days. For yearly tenancies, the notice period shall be six months.

No landlord shall take the law into his own hands by ejecting a tenant without a court order. Self-help eviction is prohibited and punishable by law.

Note: Click any card header to expand/collapse. The first card is expanded by default. Cards show the exact legal text cited in the answer.

Pipeline Summary

1. User Input - User submits question

2. Analyzing - BeUILoadingState shows during API call (backend processes: classify → retrieve → plan → draft)

3. Thinking - LoadingState shows "Thinking" while frontend processes response (only for legal questions)

4. Streaming - Answer streams in with cursor animation (~240 chars/sec)

5. Context Cards - Expandable cards show cited legal provisions

6. Action Steps - Practical steps the user can take

7. Verdict - Recommendation with escalation decision

8. Follow-ups - Clickable chips for related questions

9. Meta Row - Copy, feedback, timestamp

Total pipeline time: ~3-8 seconds depending on question complexity
Loading state visible: ~2-5 seconds (stages 2-3)
Streaming time: ~1-3 seconds (stage 4)
Remaining stages: Instant (stages 5-9)
