What the law says

Under the Lagos State Tenancy Law 2011, your landlord cannot evict you without proper notice. Section 13 requires a minimum of 7 days notice for monthly tenants.

Furthermore, Section 20 prohibits self-help eviction such as changing locks or removing your belongings. Only a court order can authorize eviction.

What you can do
  • File a complaint at the nearest Magistrate Court with your tenancy agreement and evidence
  • Contact the Lagos State Citizens Mediation Centre for free dispute resolution
  • Send a formal demand letter to your landlord requesting immediate repossession
