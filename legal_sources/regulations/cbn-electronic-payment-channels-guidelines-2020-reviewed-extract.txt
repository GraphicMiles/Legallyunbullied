3.4.6.8. If a cardholder or user notifies the bank of a transaction error, the issuer shall investigate and resolve the claim within 48 hours from the day of notification, irrespective of the dispute-resolution process of the card scheme. The acquirer must immediately respond to the issuer's request in the process.

3.4.6.9. A cardholder or user shall be given not less than five working days' notice before changes are made to the terms and conditions of the card contract.

4.5.1.13. Acquirers shall implement a fraud-management system that will detect customer usage patterns and decline or accept transactions based on defined rules.

4.5.1.14. Acquirers shall profile merchants based on the services being offered and define transaction limits.
