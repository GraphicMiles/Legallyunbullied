10.2.1. Where a Sending Entity erroneously sends value contrary to a customer's instructions due to wrong account number, wrong amount, duplication or similar bank error, and requests reversal in writing within 14 working days, the Receiving Entity shall oblige within one business day without recourse to the beneficiary, provided funds are available.

10.3. Wrong transfer due to fraud
Fraud-related wrong transfers are subject to the applicable CBN fraud and dispute-resolution procedures.
