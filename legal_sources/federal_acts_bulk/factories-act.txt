1. 	Register of factories
The Director of Factories shall keep a register of factories in which he shall cause to be entered such particulars in
relation to every factory required to be registered under this Act, as he may consider necessary or desirable.
2. 	Registration of existing factories
(1) Every person who at the commencement of this Act occupies a factory shall, within one month after such
commencement, apply for the registration of such factory to the Director of Factories by completing the
application form prescribed in the First Schedule to this Act.
